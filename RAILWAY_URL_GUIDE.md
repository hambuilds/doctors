# EXACT: How to Find Your Railway Public URL (Step by Step)

Many people get lost here. Follow **exactly**:

---

### 1. Open Railway

Go to: https://railway.app

Make sure you are logged in with the same GitHub account you used to deploy.

---

### 2. Go to Your Project

You should see a list of **Projects**.

Click on the project that contains your app (it will probably be called `doctors` or the name you gave when creating the project from GitHub).

---

### 3. You are now inside the Project

You will see one or more **boxes/cards** — these are your **Services**.

Look for the service that has your code (it usually shows "Node" or "web" or "backend").

**Click on that service box** (the big card).

---

### 4. This is the Service Page (Most Important)

On this page, look at the **very top area**.

You should see:

- A green "Deployed" status
- And right next to it or below the service name, a **big clickable link** that looks like this:

  ```
  https://freedoc-india-mvp-production.up.railway.app
  ```

This is your **public URL**.

It often has a small **copy icon** (two squares) next to it.

---

### Alternative Places the URL Appears

If you don't see it at the top:

- On the **left sidebar**, click **"Settings"**
- Scroll down to the section called **"Domains"**
- Your URL should be listed there (it will say "Public" or have the `.up.railway.app` link)

- Or click the **"Deployments"** tab on the left, then click the latest successful deployment. The URL is usually shown in the deployment details.

---

### Still Can't Find It?

Do this:

1. On the left sidebar, click **"Domains"**
2. If the list is empty, click the button **"+ New Domain"** or **"Generate Domain"**
3. Railway will create one for you automatically. It will look like:
   `https://your-project-name.up.railway.app`

---

### What the URL Looks Like

It will **always** end with `.up.railway.app`

Examples:
- `https://freedoc-india-mvp-production.up.railway.app`
- `https://doctors-production.up.railway.app`

---

### After You Find the URL

1. Copy it
2. Open it in your browser (on phone or computer)
3. Test by registering with a real phone number

**Important**: The first time it may take 10-30 seconds to load (especially on free tier).
