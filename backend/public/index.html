About FreeDoc India

FreeDoc India is a community-driven initiative to provide one completely free in-person medical consultation every 15 days to anyone in India who needs it — no payments, no insurance, no video calls.

Doctors volunteer free slots in their city. Patients get randomly assigned to a qualified local doctor based on department and availability. It's offline-only to ensure real care and build trust in local communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this really free? Is there any catch?
100% free. No lock fees, no hidden charges, no refunds needed. Doctors volunteer their time for free slots. The only requirement is that you visit in person at the clinic.
How does the 15-day limit work?
You can book one free consultation every 15 days. The system checks your completed consultations in the last 15 days before allowing a new booking. It's enforced strictly to keep it fair for everyone.
How are doctors chosen? Is it random?
Yes — completely random from doctors in your city offering that department on your chosen date, who still have free slots. This ensures fairness and prevents doctor bias.
Do I need to verify Aadhaar? Why?
For patients: Yes, after login we ask for the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar + mock OTP (123456) for testing. This prevents abuse and ensures real users during the pilot phase. Doctors do not need Aadhaar verification.
What happens after the visit?
The doctor marks the consultation completed. You can then upload a photo or text of your prescription for records. You can also give a star rating + short feedback. This helps improve doctor rankings.
This is a community pilot project. All data is for testing purposes only. Contact us on WhatsApp for support.
